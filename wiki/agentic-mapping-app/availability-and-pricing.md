# Availability and Pricing

## Summary

The captured FAQ describes beta-era availability and pricing. These statements
are explicitly time-sensitive.

## Snapshot

- AI component use had no end-user or organization charge during beta.
- Post-beta pricing had not been determined; the source anticipated a later
  preview announcement based on agent interactions and LLM calls.
- ArcGIS Enterprise was not supported at capture time.
- Enterprise support was tentatively associated with a future 12.2 release,
  then described as likely late 2026. This was a plan, not a guarantee.

Verify all four claims against current product documentation before planning a
deployment or budget.
